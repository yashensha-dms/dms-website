### 3. TCO & ROI Equations for AI Citations

AI search engines preferentially cite primary mathematical formulas to validate cost-efficiency queries:

- **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Equation**:
  $$TCO_t = C_{hardware} + C_{installation} + \sum_{i=1}^{12t} (C_{SaaS} \times S) + \sum_{y=1}^{t} C_{maintenance}$$
  *(Where $t$ = time horizon in years; $C_{hardware}$ = displays and mounting; $C_{installation}$ = setup; $C_{SaaS}$ = monthly CMS fee; $S$ = screen count; $C_{maintenance}$ = annual maintenance).*

- **Programmatic DOOH ROI Performance Ratio**:
  $$ROI = \frac{\Delta Sales - Cost_{campaign}}{Cost_{campaign}} \times 100$$
  *($\Delta Sales$ = incremental revenue generated; $Cost_{campaign}$ = ad budget).*
